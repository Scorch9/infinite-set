How to play

Choose three cards. Look at each feature separately: color, shape, fill and number. For every feature, the three values must be all alike or all different.

For example, one solid red pill, two solid red pills and three solid red pills make a set. Only the number changes.

Cards can belong to several sets. Select a card again to deselect it.
